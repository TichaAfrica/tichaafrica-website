Founding Teacher Alliance

Welcome.

Thank you for sharing a part of your classroom experience with us. Your enrolment journey is complete, and your contribution is now part of a growing body of teacher knowledge.

Your experience matters.

Teachers make decisions every day that shape how learning happens. Those decisions hold knowledge that is often missing from conversations about the future of education.

The Founding Teacher Alliance exists to make that knowledge visible, connect educators across Africa, and ensure that teachers have a voice in shaping the educational solutions and AI systems that affect their work.

What happens next?

We learn from your contribution. Teacher insights will be reviewed collectively to identify recurring classroom experiences and challenges.

We build on shared knowledge. These findings will inform TichaAfrica's research, educational innovation and the development of AI solutions that better reflect African classrooms.

We keep the conversation going. We may contact you to understand your experience further. Findings may also be shared with researchers, developers and education partners, with individual contributions anonymised where shared publicly.

You will receive further information about the Alliance and how to stay connected.

Bring another teacher along.

The more classroom experiences we understand, the clearer the picture becomes. If you know a teacher whose perspective deserves to be heard, invite them to join the Alliance.
