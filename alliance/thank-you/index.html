Welcome. We're glad you're here.

You've joined a growing community of African teachers bringing their knowledge, experiences and ideas together to shape the future of education.

The future of education cannot be shaped meaningfully without the people who understand classrooms best. That is why your presence here matters.

Together, we are building a collective voice for African teachers, one capable of influencing educational innovation, research and the development of AI that understands our realities.

This is bigger than any one teacher or classroom. It is a necessary contribution to the future we want to see.

And this is just the beginning.

We'll keep you informed as the Alliance grows, share what emerges from our collective contributions and let you know how to stay involved.
