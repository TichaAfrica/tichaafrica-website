Your experience matters

Every classroom holds knowledge worth understanding.

Teachers make decisions every day that shape how learners understand, participate and grow. Much of that knowledge remains inside the classroom. We want to help bring it into the wider conversation about education and artificial intelligence.

Why we are asking

TichaAfrica believes that teachers should help shape the systems being built for education. Your experiences can help us recognise recurring classroom realities, develop research questions and inform educational solutions that reflect the contexts in which African learners live and learn.

Let's begin with one experience.

It does not have to be extraordinary. It only needs to be real.

Your classroom insight

Think of a real moment in your teaching when something did not go as expected, a learner struggled, or you had to make a decision that changed how learning happened. What happened, and how did you respond?

Describe the situation in your own words. You may also share what you learnt from it or what you wish an educational tool or AI system understood about that situation.

Write in any language. There is no perfect answer. 0 characters
1

Be specific. A particular classroom moment is more useful than a broad statement about education.

2

Use your natural voice. You do not need academic language or technical knowledge of AI.

3

Protect learners' privacy. Please do not include learners' names, contact details or other identifying personal information.

What happens to your contribution?

TichaAfrica may analyse contributions collectively to identify shared classroom experiences and inform research, educational innovation and AI development. We may also use insights to guide further conversations with educators and share findings with researchers, developers, education institutions and potential partners. Where individual contributions are published or shared publicly, they will be anonymised. Your contribution is part of a wider effort to make teachers' knowledge visible and useful.
