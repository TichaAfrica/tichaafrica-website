A place for teachers at the table

Your classroom knowledge belongs in the future of education.

The Founding Teacher Alliance brings teachers together to contribute real classroom experience and help shape AI and educational solutions for African schools.

This is a contribution, not a course registration.

You do not need to be an AI expert. You are here because you understand the realities of teaching and learning. That knowledge is essential to building solutions that work.

How the Alliance works

The Alliance is where teachers come together, share knowledge and begin contributing to a wider effort to shape the future of education.

01   A community of educators

Connect with teachers who believe classroom knowledge should have a voice in decisions about education and artificial intelligence.

02   Dialogue and shared knowledge

Contribute your ideas, experiences and perspectives. Learn from other educators and help bring important classroom realities into view.

03   Research and public insight

Your contributions help TichaAfrica identify emerging questions, understand shared challenges and build knowledge that can inform research, educational innovation and responsible AI.

04   Pathways to deeper participation

As TichaAfrica develops, opportunities for further learning, research, media contributions, collaboration and professional engagement will create pathways for teachers to extend their influence beyond the classroom.
