A place for teachers at the table

Your classroom knowledge belongs in the future of education.

The Founding Teacher Alliance brings teachers together to contribute real classroom experience and help shape AI and educational solutions for African schools.

This is a contribution, not a course registration.

You do not need to be an AI expert. You are here because you understand the realities of teaching and learning. That knowledge is essential to building solutions that work.

How the Alliance works

01   Share what happens in your classroom
Tell us what works, what gets in the way and what is missing.
02   Help us recognise shared challenges
We look for patterns across teachers, schools and communities.
03   Inform what gets developed
Teacher insight helps guide future tools, learning resources and educational solutions.
04   Stay connected as the work develops
The Alliance creates room for continued exchange, collaboration and participation.
