From the Classroom
“Halfway through the explanation I changed it. Not because I planned to — because three faces in the second row told me it was not landing. No system has ever asked me how I know.”

Amara teaches secondary school in northern Nigeria. She manages multilingual classrooms, unstable infrastructure, uneven access to learning materials, and constantly shifting emotional realities inside a single school day.

When asked what she does, she says: teaching.

What she actually does is one of the most sophisticated forms of professional intelligence the modern educational world still does not know how to measure.

Amara O. Secondary School Teacher · Kano, Nigeria
