African Classroom Intelligence

African classroom intelligence is becoming one of the most consequential forces in the future of learning.
TichaAfrica exists to ensure Africa helps shape what comes next.

TichaAfrica is building the intellectual, professional, and institutional infrastructure through which the knowledge developed inside African classrooms becomes visible to the systems now shaping African education.

Somewhere in Africa right now, a teacher is rebuilding a lesson across three languages so a child does not get left behind.

Positioning
Educational systems serving African learners cannot remain intellectually incomplete. The teachers who understand African classrooms most deeply must become part of the foundations shaping how those systems are designed.
Thought leadership

The future of African learning will be shaped either with African classroom intelligence — or without the accuracy African learners deserve.

TichaAfrica is building a continental intellectual movement around classroom intelligence: documenting it, refining it, protecting it, and ensuring it becomes institutionally consequential in the systems shaping African education.
